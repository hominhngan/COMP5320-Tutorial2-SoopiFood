# Interview guide (round 1): Restaurant manager, SoopiFood discovery

**Format:** semi-structured, 45-60 minutes · **Stakeholder:** restaurant manager (runs service, not the owner)
**Goal:** understand current delivery workflows, pain points and needs. No features are being validated.

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)

**Q1.** Tell me about your role and what a typical shift looks like for you, from opening to close.
- Probe: How do you juggle delivery orders with everything else you are responsible for during a shift?
- Probe: Which parts of the shift take most of your attention?

## 2. Current workflow (15 min)

**Q2.** Walk me through what happens from the moment a delivery order arrives until the food leaves the restaurant.
- Probe: Who sees the order first, and how does it reach the kitchen?
- Probe: Can you show me or describe the last order you handled today?

**Q3.** How do you keep track of where each delivery order is while the kitchen is preparing it?
- Probe: What do you look at, or who do you ask, to know an order is ready?

## 3. Pain points (10 min)

**Q4.** What are the most frustrating challenges you face with delivery orders during your busiest shifts?
- Probe: Tell me about the last time an order went wrong. What happened next?
- Probe: What do you do when more orders arrive than the kitchen can handle?

**Q5.** When a dish runs out or an item on the menu is unavailable, how is that handled for delivery orders?
- Probe: How do customers and the platform find out?

## 4. Coordination with riders and customers (10 min)

**Q6.** Describe how a typical handover to a rider happens at your restaurant.
- Probe: What happens when a rider arrives before the food is ready, or the food is ready and no rider is there?
- Probe: How do you confirm the right rider takes the right order?

**Q7.** How do complaints about delivery orders reach you, and what do you do with them?
- Probe: Walk me through the most recent complaint you handled.

## 5. Success and priorities (8 min)

**Q8.** At the end of a good shift, how do you know delivery went well?
- Probe: What does your owner or area manager look at to judge your delivery performance?

**Q9.** If you could change one thing about how delivery orders work in your restaurant today, what would it be and why?
- Probe: What would that change allow you or your team to do differently?

## 6. Wrap-up (5 min)

**Q10.** Is there anything about running delivery during service that we have not asked about but that you think matters?
- Probe: Who else in your team should we talk to?

## If time runs short

Cut **Q5** (menu availability) and **Q8** (success measures) first; Q2 and Q4 usually surface both topics. Keep Q2, Q4 and Q6, the core of the workflow and the handover.
