# Personas: Restaurant managers (SoopiFood)

Sources: interviews I1–I8 and observations O1–O2 in `03a_interview_notes.md` (simulated).

## Persona 1: Sam, the Short-Staffed Night Manager

Sam runs the evening shift with a very small team and covers host, cashier and expediting at once. Sam is often away from the tablet and needs it to keep working without constant attention. Missed or cancelled orders are what the owner judges Sam on.

**Goals**
- Keep the cancellation rate low so the owner trusts the evening shift [I1, I8]
- Get every delivery order out correctly without extra staff [I1, I3, I5]
- Keep customers satisfied even when the kitchen is under pressure [I5, O2]

**Behaviours**
- Covers several roles at once and is away from the tablet most of the time [I1, I8, O1]
- Accepts orders only when back at the counter [I8, O1]
- Reads orders aloud or relays them on paper to the kitchen [I1, O1]
- Estimates ready times personally instead of trusting the tablet [I5]

**Pain points**
- Orders time out and are cancelled while Sam is serving tables [I1, O1]
- Marking an item as sold out is slow and goes through platform support [I1, I8, O2]
- No shared view of which orders are ready or picked up [O1, O2]
- Refunds and complaints need long calls or chats with the platform [I5, O2]

**Context**
- Evening shift with 1–3 staff; peak 6–9pm [I1, I3, I8]
- Tablet on the counter, read from a distance while busy [I3, O1]
- Shared Wi-Fi that drops at dinner time [I3, O1]

## Persona 2: Thu, the Process-Driven Franchise Manager

Thu runs a high-volume franchise shift by the book: checklists, procedures and fixed roles. Thu cares about throughput and accuracy because the franchise office and the platform ranking measure both.

**Goals**
- Keep a high position in the platform ranking by being fast and reliable [I2]
- Meet franchise accuracy standards on every order [I7]
- Keep the delivery line predictable at peak [I2, I4]

**Behaviours**
- Has a dedicated staff member for delivery orders [I2]
- Checks every bag against a checklist before handover [I7]
- Prints tickets for the kitchen rather than reading the tablet [I2]

**Pain points**
- Riders who do not show up leave food waiting and cold [I7]
- Riders go to the wrong counter or take the wrong bag [I2, I3]
- Complaints arrive the next day, too late to fix [I7]

**Context**
- Chain or franchise branch; delivery is a large share of sales [I2, I7]
- Procedures set by head office [I7]
- Kitchen works from printed tickets rather than the tablet [I2]

## Persona 3: Quang, the Dine-In-First Floor Manager

Quang treats delivery as a side business that must not disturb dine-in guests. Quang delegates the tablet and wants delivery to stay out of the way.

**Goals**
- Protect the dine-in experience [I6]
- Control delivery volume when the restaurant is busy [I4, I6]
- Stay visible to customers on the app even while slowing orders [I4]

**Behaviours**
- Delegates order acceptance to a host [I6]
- Pauses delivery at peak by turning the tablet off [I4]
- Asks riders to wait outside [I6]

**Pain points**
- Riders waiting in the dining room disturb guests [I6]
- The only way to slow orders is to disappear from the app [I4]
- The tablet shows nothing after acceptance, so the kitchen tracks orders on a whiteboard [I4]

**Context**
- Delivery is a small share of sales [I6]
- Busiest period is dinner, 6–9pm [I4]
- A host, not the manager, handles the tablet [I6]

## What makes each persona distinct

**Sam** is limited by people: too few hands, away from the tablet. **Thu** is limited by process and volume: everything must follow the procedure at speed. **Quang** is limited by priorities: delivery comes after dine-in.
