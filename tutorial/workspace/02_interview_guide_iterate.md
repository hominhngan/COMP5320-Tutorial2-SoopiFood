# Interview guide (round 2, neutral wording): Restaurant manager, SoopiFood discovery

**Format:** semi-structured, 45–60 minutes · **Stakeholder:** restaurant manager (runs service, not the owner)
**Goal:** understand current delivery workflows, pain points and needs. No features are being validated.

## My review of round 1

I read every question in `01_interview_guide.md` as the manager would. These four were leading, so I asked the agent to rewrite them in neutral wording:

| Question | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 probe "How do you juggle delivery orders…" | "Juggle" assumes the manager feels stretched | Ask where delivery fits in the shift |
| Q4 "most frustrating challenges" | Pushes the manager to complain | Ask how delivery affects the busiest shifts, good or bad |
| Q4 probe "the last time an order went wrong" | Assumes orders go wrong | Ask about the last order that needed their attention |
| Q9 "If you could change one thing…" | Assumes something needs to change | Ask what works and what does not |

## 1. Warm-up (5 min)

**Q1.** Tell me about your role and what a typical shift looks like for you, from opening to close.
- Probe: Where do delivery orders fit into the rest of your responsibilities during a shift?
- Probe: Which parts of the shift take most of your attention?

## 2. Current workflow (15 min)

**Q2.** Walk me through what happens from the moment a delivery order arrives until the food leaves the restaurant.
- Probe: Who sees the order first, and how does it reach the kitchen?
- Probe: Can you describe the last order you handled today?

**Q3.** How do you keep track of where each delivery order is while the kitchen is preparing it?
- Probe: What do you look at, or who do you ask, to know an order is ready?

## 3. Pain points (10 min)

**Q4.** Thinking about your busiest shifts, how do delivery orders affect the way the restaurant runs?
- Probe: Tell me about the last delivery order that needed you to step in. What happened?
- Probe: How does the kitchen deal with the number of orders at peak times?

**Q5.** When a dish runs out or a menu item is unavailable, how is that handled for delivery orders?
- Probe: How do customers and the platform find out?

## 4. Coordination with riders and customers (10 min)

**Q6.** Describe how a typical handover to a rider happens at your restaurant.
- Probe: How do the timing of the food and the arrival of the rider line up?
- Probe: How do you confirm the right rider takes the right order?

**Q7.** How do comments or complaints about delivery orders reach you, and what do you do with them?
- Probe: Walk me through the most recent one you handled.

## 5. Success and priorities (8 min)

**Q8.** At the end of a shift, how do you know whether delivery went well?
- Probe: What does your owner or area manager look at to judge delivery performance?

**Q9.** Which parts of how delivery works today would you keep as they are, and which work less well for you?
- Probe: What would be different for you or your team if those parts worked better?

## 6. Wrap-up (5 min)

**Q10.** Is there anything about running delivery during service that we have not asked about but that you think matters?
- Probe: Who else in your team should we talk to?

## If time runs short

Cut **Q5** (menu availability) and **Q8** (success measures) first; Q2 and Q4 usually surface both topics. Keep Q2, Q4 and Q6, the core of the workflow and the handover.
